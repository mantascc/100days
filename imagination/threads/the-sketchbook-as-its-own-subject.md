# What
The practice keeps building instruments for looking at itself. Not
documentation of individual pieces — apparatus whose subject is the corpus as
a whole: an index that computes its own thumbnails, a video wall of every
sketch recorded by an agent, a concept graph, a timeline of the Imagination
layer, field notes on a style nobody designed.

This was never planned and went unnamed for months, which is the usual
signature of a real thread. It is also the thread most likely to be mistaken
for procrastination, and occasionally is — building a viewer for the work is a
way of not making the work. The distinction that seems to hold: an instrument
earns its place when it shows something no individual sketch could, and fails
when it only rearranges what was already visible.

# Charge
High — it is most of what the last two months have been, and it now has
apparatus outside the sketchbook proper. Also the thread with the sharpest
failure mode, and the obvious target for a Break: the practice could go a
month making nothing that refers to itself.

# Prompts
- An instrument that reads the corpus and reports what it *cannot* see — coverage holes rather than coverage.
- The index as an agent: it does not list the work, it argues about it.
- A viewer that shows the sketchbook's drift over time as a single continuous field, with no per-piece boundaries at all.
