# What
Build Conrad's apophenic arc — Trema, Apophänie, Anastrophe — as a four-state instrument rather than a representation. The three terms are one membrane dissolving: outside-but-loaded → figure-resolves → pattern-addresses-you. The piece adds the fourth term Conrad's patients never had: **un-anastrophe**, the controlled exit ("it was never about me"). The valve is the whole aesthetic. Same field of dots, one boolean — `valveOpen`. With it open, you slide back to Trema after the address: sublime, the cosmos indifferent. Welded shut, you stay inside the address indefinitely: paranoid, the For You feed.

Each phase has its own clock: Trema is **durative** (swell, atmospheric), Apophany is **punctual** (snap, zero duration), Anastrophe is **recursive** (spiral, feeds itself). Un-anastrophe is deflating — the mechanism becomes visible.
