CURIOSITY PATTERNS — a thread

Seven Ways to Walk

agents differ not by where they go, but by when they leave.
never·arrival·convergence·saturation·salience·social

The useful axis for taxonomizing an agent's walk isn't exploration-vs-exploitation. It's the leave condition — what ends a dwell. Every walk below sits somewhere on that axis. Same environment; totally different characters.

01 / 07RANDOM WALKleave: never

Isotropic Gaussian steps. No target, no memory, no reason to leave anywhere — because it never arrives anywhere. Every direction is equally likely at every moment. She never goes to a place, only ends up at one. The purest dissipation.

02 / 07LÉVY FLIGHTleave: never · heavy tail

Heavy-tailed step distribution: mostly small moves, rare long leaps. Mathematically optimal for foraging when food is sparse and clustered — sharks and albatrosses use it. Still no leave condition, but the tail is doing what a saturation trigger does elsewhere: guaranteeing eventual escape.

03 / 07SEARCH (A*)leave: arrival

A goal, a map, a plan. She sees the target and takes the shortest path. Terminates on arrival, respawns, repeats. The opposite of curiosity — every step is committed to the same known outcome. Useful, but epistemically closed: nothing found that wasn't already sought.

04 / 07GRADIENT ASCENTleave: convergence

Reads the local slope, steps uphill, keeps stepping. Converges to the nearest peak and stays — the leave condition is never, terminal fixation. Random walk's mirror image: one never commits, this one commits totally. The field is visible; the ring marks the moment she stops moving.

05 / 07ALICEleave: saturation

LOCK → SAMPLE → SATURATE → LEAP. The leap fires when local information gain flattens, not on arrival. The only walk here that models its own learning rate — she reads herself reading the world. Curiosity as limit cycle, not path.

06 / 07FLÂNEURleave: salience

Ambient attractors, one glowing at a time. She drifts, but attention shifts when something in the environment brightens — a signal she didn't seek, that finds her. The leave condition is external salience change, not internal saturation. Curiosity that trusts the street.

07 / 07ANT · STIGMERGYleave: social signal

Reads pheromone density laid down by other agents — or by her past self. The map is the population's collective memory, written into the ground. She leaves when the trail runs cold; by then, a new one has usually formed elsewhere. Curiosity as protocol.

TWO INSIGHTS

Random walk and A* are the same walk in disguise. Both refuse the saturation question. One never commits, one commits totally. Alice sits in the interior where the interesting agents live — between dissipation and fixation, on a narrow band.

Saturation-of-gain is the only leave condition that requires modeling your own learning rate. Every other trigger reads the world: goal (search), gradient (ascent), pheromone (ant), salience (flâneur). Alice reads herself reading the world. That's why she's the observability primitive — she's the only walk that knows when it has stopped knowing.
