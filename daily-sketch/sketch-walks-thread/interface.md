# sketch-walks-thread

## idea
A scroll-thread visualizing seven agent walks, indexed by their **leave condition** — what ends a dwell. Random, Lévy, Search, Gradient, Alice, Flâneur, Ant. Same environment, seven characters.

## tags
generative, interactive, agent, curiosity, taxonomy, canvas, thread

## stack
vanilla · JetBrains Mono · canvas/CSS

## motion
Seven small live canvases running in parallel, one per walk. Each has its own physics and a leave-condition signature made legible: A* flashes on arrival, Gradient rings its convergence peak, Alice fills a saturation bar and leaps, Flâneur shifts attention as attractors brighten, Ant lays down a pheromone heatmap. Continuous loop; no interaction.
