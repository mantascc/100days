# sketch-relationship-types

## idea
A specimen sheet of relationship itself: sixteen live micro-simulations, each
isolating one type of edge — directed, mutual, asymmetric, ephemeral, latent,
cyclic, transitive, broken; hierarchy, hub, peer mesh, broker; dependency,
mutualism, parasitism, similarity-without-connection. The thesis line: a
relationship is a constraint on how two things change together.

## tags
graph, taxonomy, edges, generative, interactive, specimen-sheet

## stack
vanilla · IBM Plex Mono · canvas/CSS

## motion
Each cell runs a tiny continuous simulation: pulses travel along edges to show
direction and reciprocity; edges flicker, decay, or fade in with proximity to
show time; nodes grow, shrink, or dim to show what the edge does to its ends. Pulses are comets with tails; nodes glow
when a pulse arrives so cause and effect read at a glance. No interaction.
