# sketch-audio-agents-grid

## idea
A 3×3 grid of miniature agent worlds — nine distinct movement rules, all driven by a single microphone signal.

## tags
generative, audio, agents, variations, canvas

## stack
vanilla · IBM Plex Mono · canvas + WebAudio

## motion
Silence: every cell breathes slowly at its own baseline.
Sound: all nine wake in their own way — drift, orbit, flow field, wave, spiral, pulse, lattice, chase, alignment.
The shared envelope has fast attack and slow release, so the swarm blooms on a sound and sustains as it fades — the hypnotic start/stop is uniform across every variant.
