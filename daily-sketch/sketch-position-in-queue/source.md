# POSITION IN QUEUE

## Premise

A human moves through a system of services. Forms, tickets, waiting rooms.
The zine ends when he realizes he is alone in it.

**The inversion:** Kafka's horror is that *someone is there* — hidden, judging.
This horror is that **no one is**. Not surveilled. Unwitnessed.
The system isn't cruel. It's uninhabited.

## Form

9-panel grid, uniform, never broken until the last.
Black-first. One accent. Protagonist is **V.**, no face given until panel 9.

| # | Panel | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | V. hands a form across a counter. Counter is very wide. | "Take a number." |
| 2 | Ticket: **0001** | — |
| 3 | Waiting room. Chairs. All empty. | — |
| 4 | Phone screen. Chat bubble arrives instantly. | "Someone will be with you shortly." |
| 5 | Same room, different room. Identical. New form. Name: **V. ---AS** | — |
| 6 | Hands filling a field labeled *Reason for existence (optional)* | — |
| 7 | The counter again. Behind it: a chair, pushed in. Dust. | — |
| 8 | Screen: "Your case is progressing." Timestamp identical to panel 4. | — |
| 9 | Wide. V. alone, small, centered. **Panel border gone.** No gutter. The white keeps going. | "Position in queue: 1" |

## Devices

- **Name erosion** — V. loses a letter per panel. By 9, blank.
- **The gutter as queue** — white space between panels widens 1px per row. Panel 9 is all gutter.
- **The reply is always instant.** Never a delay. Instant is worse — nobody read it.
- **No antagonist. No villain panel.** If you draw a bureaucrat, you've lost.

## Tati — Playtime (the operative reference)

What to steal, mechanically:

1. **No close-ups.** Playtime has almost none. The camera never gets near enough
   to grant interiority. Keep V. at medium-long in every panel. Panel 9 lands
   because you never gave him a face to lose.
2. **Deep focus, distributed action.** Tati refuses to tell you where to look.
   Panels *are* a pointing finger — so make each one a wide evenly-lit box with
   three or four small events, none of them V.'s. He's an element, not a subject.
3. **The glass.** Playtime's cubicles: see everyone, reach no one. Transparency
   as the *mechanism* of isolation, not its opposite. Draw the waiting room in
   glass. He sees the other applicants. They see him. Nobody looks.
4. **Tati is funny.** This is the discipline. Bureaucracy horror that knows it's
   horror is just goth. Play it as comedy — the chairs squeak, the pen is
   chained, the ticket dispenser is beautifully designed — and let the last
   panel arrive without warning.

## Abe — the correction

*The Box Man* is a man who **chooses** the box. V. doesn't choose.

Closer: ***The Woman in the Dunes*** — a man kept not by walls but by the daily
labor of maintaining his own confinement. He shovels sand every night or he's
buried. The system requires nothing but his participation. Nobody guards him.

That's the form-filling. **V. isn't trapped. V. is shoveling.**

## Other references

- Graeber, *The Utopia of Rules* — "interpretive labor": the powerless must
  imagine the institution's inner life. The institution imagines nothing back.
- Kafka, *The Castle* (over *The Trial*) — no verdict, just permanent adjacency.
- Melville, *Bartleby* — refusal that costs nothing and changes nothing.
- Yuichi Yokoyama, *Travel* — comics as pure procedure, zero interiority.
- Thomas Ott — wordless scratchboard, black-first.
- Chris Ware — the grid as filing system. The page *is* the form.
- *Papers, Please* — bureaucracy where you're the clerk, not the applicant.

## Open threads

- Alternate ending: V. realizes he is the one processing the forms.
- Alternate mode: draw the whole thing as an **instruction manual**. Second
  person. "Take a number. Wait. If your name is called, it is not yours."
- Back cover sound cue: one sustained note, no resolution. Or nothing.
