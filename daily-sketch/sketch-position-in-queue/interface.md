# Position in Queue

This sketch tests a raster-first workflow: save the complete text seed, generate
a single image from it, iterate the bitmap, and use the web surface as a viewer
rather than the primary generative medium.

## Generation Prompt V2

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: project sketch image, second-pass generated bitmap for an
experimental web sketch
Primary request: Create a more surreal, more office-bound, brutalist post-Soviet
version of "Position in Queue": bureaucracy horror through absence, not
surveillance. The applicant is trapped inside an uninhabited administrative
office complex, not a clean service center. The system is empty, massive, and
self-maintaining.
Style/medium: black-first scratchboard ink zine page, high contrast, rough xerox
texture, handmade comic page. Brutalist post-Soviet administrative architecture:
raw concrete, low suspended ceilings, fluorescent tube grids, heavy doors,
peeling institutional paint, linoleum floors, laminate desks, filing cabinets,
rubber plants, wall clocks, numbered office doors, paper stacks, stamp pads,
radiators, coat racks, frosted glass partitions. One restrained accent color:
faded Soviet red used only for stamps, ticket numbers, tiny indicator lights,
and office signage.
Composition/framing: 9-panel comic grid, but make the spaces stranger and more
architectural. No close-ups. Wide Tati-like camera distance in every panel. The
applicant remains small, faceless, and almost swallowed by office geometry.
Panels should feel like rooms inside rooms: corridors branching into identical
corridors, office cubicles behind glass, waiting chairs in absurd rows, counters
with no clerk, a desk placed in the middle of a cavernous concrete hall, a ticket
dispenser mounted on a concrete pillar, forms sliding out of wall slots. Final
panel: border gone, applicant tiny in a vast blank administrative white, like an
office floor plan dissolving into paper.
Scene/backdrop: surreal post-Soviet ministry interior, brutalist municipal
building, empty bureaucratic offices, endless corridors, numbered doors, frosted
glass, concrete stairwells, fluorescent silence, institutional furniture. More
office than service counter.
Subject: one faceless applicant in a dark coat, medium-long or tiny, filling
forms and waiting; no visible face, no interiority, no heroic pose.
Text: avoid large readable text. If small text appears, only use tiny fragments
like "0001", "1", "KAB", "FORM", blank form lines, or red stamp marks. Do not
attempt paragraphs.
Constraints: no bureaucrat, no clerk, no antagonist, no villain, no staff member,
no surveillance camera emphasis, no monster, no gore, no dramatic noir shadows.
Keep the horror procedural, empty, slightly absurd, and architectural.
Avoid: clean modern glass service center look, dramatic gothic horror, cyberpunk,
cinematic action, readable paragraphs, faces looking at camera, logos, watermark,
extra dominant characters.

## Generation Prompt V1

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: project sketch image for an experimental web sketch
Primary request: Create a single black-first zine/comics page inspired by the
text "Position in Queue": bureaucracy horror through absence, not surveillance.
A human applicant V. moves through empty service counters, ticket machines,
glass waiting rooms, forms, and automated screens, ending alone in white space.
Style/medium: monochrome scratchboard ink drawing, black-first, high contrast,
rough zine texture, Thomas Ott-like scratch marks, Chris Ware-like strict grid
discipline, Tati Playtime-like wide distant camera and glass office geometry.
One restrained accent color: sickly ticket-red used only for tiny numbers,
stamps, or UI dots.
Composition/framing: 9-panel grid, uniform small panels at first, widening white
gutters toward the bottom; final ninth panel loses its border and dissolves into
a wide white field. No close-ups. Keep the applicant medium-long or tiny in every
panel, no visible face. Use deep-focus wide boxes with evenly lit bureaucratic
furniture, transparent glass partitions, empty counters, identical rooms,
chained pen, ticket dispenser, empty chair behind counter, dust.
Scene/backdrop: empty modern municipal service center rendered as glass,
counters, chairs, forms, ticket screens, fluorescent light, filing-system page
layout.
Subject: one faceless applicant in a dark coat, gradually becoming less
named/less present, maintaining his own confinement through form-filling.
Text: avoid large readable text. If small text appears, only use brief fragments
like "0001", "1", or blank form lines; do not attempt paragraphs.
Constraints: no bureaucrat, no antagonist, no villain, no staff member behind
the counter, no close-up face, no cinematic shadows, no surveillance cameras, no
dramatic monster imagery. The horror should feel procedural, funny, and empty.
Avoid: readable paragraphs, extra characters dominating the scene, noir
detective style, gothic horror, blood, gore, monsters, faces looking at camera,
logos, watermark.
